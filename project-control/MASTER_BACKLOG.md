## Rejected / superseded principles

- Do not build a universal graph engine.
- Do not build fake execution runtimes.
- Do not use authored DOM selectors/pixel coordinates as semantic APIs.
- Do not fork shared infrastructure inside consumers to make one experiment pass.
- Do not treat a visually attractive one-off as proof of a reusable renderer family.
